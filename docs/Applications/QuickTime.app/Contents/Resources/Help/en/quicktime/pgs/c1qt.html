Opening and Playing Movies in QuickTime Player

You can use QuickTime Player to play video, audio, texts, images.

To open a movie, do one of the following:

  • In Finder, double-click the file or drag it to the QuickTime Player application icon.
  • Choose File > Open and select the file.

QuickTime Player has onscreen controls similar to those found on CD players and DVD players. Use the controls to play, pause, rewind, fast-forward, jump to the beginning or end, and adjust the volume of a movie or audio. You can also use the controls to move forward or backward in a movie.

Some movies have chapter lists you can use to jump to a specific point in a movie.

You can drag the handle in the lower-right corner of the window to resize the viewing space.
