Setting Audio and Video Effects

For any QuickTime Player movie with an audio track, you can adjust the right/left balance, as well as the volume, bass, and treble levels. For any movie, you can also set playback options such as speed and playback rate (the jog shuttle).

To set audio and video effects:

  1. Choose Window > A/V Controls.
  2. Drag a slider to adjust the setting.
