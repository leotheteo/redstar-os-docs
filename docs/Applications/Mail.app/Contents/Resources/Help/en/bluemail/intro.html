Chapter 1. Introduction

Since most people do not read documentation anyway, here is a collection of the most helpful tips:

  • You do not have to use your mouse to use Mail. Everything can be done by using Keyboard Shortcuts.

  • Although Mail can be considered reliable you should keep backups of your messages, i.e. just copy the files and folders in ~/Mail (including the hidden ones that start with a dot) to a safe place.
