Rotating an image

If an image isn’t oriented the way you want it, you can rotate it in 90-degree increments, or flip it horizontally or vertically. You can rotate and flip an image several times until it’s correctly oriented.

To rotate an image, do one of the following:

■

To turn the selected image 90 degrees in either direction, choose Tools > Rotate Left or Tools > Rotate Right

■

To flip the image horizontally or vertically, choose Tools > Flip Horizontal or Tools > Flip Vertical
