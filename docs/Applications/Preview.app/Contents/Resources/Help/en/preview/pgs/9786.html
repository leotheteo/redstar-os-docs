Zooming in and out

You can change the image or page view of a PDF file in several ways. For example, you can zoom in to get a close-up of an image or zoom out to see a larger area of the image.

Here are some ways to zoom in and out:

■

To zoom in or out, choose View > Zoom In or View > Zoom Out

■

To see the image or page in its original size, choose View > Actual Size

■

To view an image or page at a size you specify, resize the Preview window to the size you want, then choose View > Automatically Resize . The image resizes to fit the window size.

■

To zoom to a particular section of a PDF document, choose Tools > Select Tool, select the section, and then choose View > Zoom To Selection

You cannot save changes to the way you view an image or page.
