About Preview

In addition to viewing PDF and image files, you can alter them.

Viewing PDF and image files
View many types of files, including PDF, images. Use bookmarks to find your files and then use a slideshow to show them off. And when you’re viewing a PDF file, you can move around it quickly by searching it or using its table of contents.
 
Editing PDF and image files
Crop, rotate, or flip an image or the pages of a PDF file. With a PDF file, you can remove pages.
