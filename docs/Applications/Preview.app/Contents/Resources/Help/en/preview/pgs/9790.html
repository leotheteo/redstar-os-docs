Scrolling PDF pages

When you open a PDF file with multiple pages in Preview, you select the page you want to view by clicking it in the sidebar. By default, you can’t use the vertical scroll bar to view the next or previous pages of the document. However, you can change this.

To use the vertical scroll bar to scroll through the document’s pages, do one of the following:

■

Choose View > PDF Display > Single Page Continuous to view one page at a time.

■

Choose View > PDF Display > Two Pages Continuous to view two pages side by side.
