Displaying your calculations

You can display your calculations in a list as you enter them. You can then print or save the list to keep a record of your calculations.

■

To display a calculating list of your calculations, choose Window > Show Paper Tape, [Ctrl + T] .

■

To print the calculations progress, choose File > Print Tape.

■

To save the calculations progress, choose File > Save Tape As. Type a name for your tape, choose where to save it, and click Save.

■

To hide the Paper Tape window, choose Window > Hide Paper Tape.
