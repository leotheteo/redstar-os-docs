Converting units of measure

You can use Calculator to convert measurement values from one unit of measure to another. For example, you can convert yards to meters or inches, or pounds to grams or ounces.

To convert units of measurement:

Enter the original value in Calculator.

Choose a measurement category (such as Area or length, pressure) from the Convert menu.

Choose the original unit of measure from the From pop-up menu and the unit of measure you want to convert to from the To pop-up menu.
