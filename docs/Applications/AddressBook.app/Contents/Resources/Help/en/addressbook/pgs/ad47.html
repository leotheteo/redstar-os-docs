Deleting a contact from your address book

When you delete a contact from your address book, the contact is also removed from all groups.

  1. Select All in the Group column and then select the contact or contacts you want to remove.
  2. Press the Delete key.

If you want to remove a contact from a group but keep the contact in your address book, select the group, then select the contact and press the Delete key. Click "Remove from Group."
