Setting up your contact information

  1. Select your card in Address Book. Make sure it contains your email address, phone number, and so on.
  2. Choose Card / Make This My Card.
  3. Add your picture to this card to have it show up in other applications that use Address Book.

You can also choose Card /"Go to My Card" to see your contact information.
