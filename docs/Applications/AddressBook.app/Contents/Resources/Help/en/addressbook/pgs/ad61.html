Viewing multiple contacts or groups

You can switch between viewing single contacts, or a list of all contacts and groups in your address book.

Choose View / Card Only or View / "Card and Columns."

When you are viewing cards only, use the arrow buttons at the bottom of the window to view the next or previous card.

You can also double-click a name in the Name column to open the card in a separate window.
