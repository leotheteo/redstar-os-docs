Adding contacts to a group

You can create a group and add contacts to it to help keep your address book organized.

To add a contact to a group:

  1. If there is not a group already, click the Add (+) button at the bottom of the Group column. Give the group a name.
  2. Click All at the top of the Group column.
  3. Select a contact in the Name column and drag it to the group name.

You can put contacts in more than one group.
