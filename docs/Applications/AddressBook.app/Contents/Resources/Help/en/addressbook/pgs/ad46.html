Deleting a contact from a group

When you delete a contact from a group, you can remove that contact from the group only, or remove it from your address book completely.

  1. Select the group the contact belongs to.
  2. Select the contact you want to remove.
  3. Choose Edit / Delete Card to remove the contact completely, or Edit / Remove From Group to remove the contact from the group but keep the contact in your address book.

You can select more than one contact to delete.
