Adding pictures to your Address Book

  1. Select the person's card in Address Book and click Edit.
  2. Double-click the picture area.
  3. Click Choose and locate a picture to use. You can also drag an image to the picture area, or if you have a video camera connected to your computer, you can take a video snapshot to use as a picture.

Use the size slider to enlarge or crop the picture you use.

The person doesn't see the picture you assign to his or her card; it's for your use only.
