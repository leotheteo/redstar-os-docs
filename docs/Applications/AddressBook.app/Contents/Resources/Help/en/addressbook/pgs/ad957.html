Keeping track of birthday, anniversaries, and other important dates

You can use Address Book to remember the birthdays of your friends and family. You can also add date fields to remember anniversaries and other important dates.

  1. Select a person's card in Address Book.
  2. Choose Card / Add Field / Birthday. If you are adding a date other than a birthday, choose Dates.
  3. Enter the person's birth date and click the Edit button.

If you don't specify a year, the current year is used.
