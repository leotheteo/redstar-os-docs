Creating a to-do item

You can keep track of things you need to do by creating items in a to-do list. After you complete an item, you can check it off in your to-do list. You can also add an alarm to your to-do items.

If you use Mac OS X Mail to send and receive email, your to-do items are listed in the To Do box under Reminders in the Mail window.

Note:If your calendar is administered through a Microsoft Exchange Server 2007, you can add to-do items only to the Tasks calendar.

To create a to-do item:

  1. Select a calendar in the Calendars list, and then double-click the blank space in the To Do list at the right side of the kCal window, or choose File > New To Do.

    If you don’t see the To Do list, click the button in the lower-right corner of the window (looks like a pushpin).

  2. Type a name for the new to-do item, and then press the Return key.

  3. To choose other settings for the to-do item (for example, to assign a due date or priority level), double-click the to-do item.

    • To prioritize the item, choose a rating from the “priority” pop-up menu.

      The priority icon appears to the right of the item in the To Do list. One bar means “low,” two bars mean “medium,” and three bars mean “high.”

    • To assign the item a due date, select the “due date” checkbox, and then select the month, day, and year in the adjacent date fields.

      If the due date passes before you mark the to-do item as completed, an alert icon appears beside the item. Mark an item as completed by selecting the checkbox next to it in the To Do list, or by selecting the “completed” checkbox in the editor.

    • To receive a message or hear a sound before the due date passes, set an alarm.

    • If you want to remember a particular website that’s associated with this to-do item, type it into the “url” field.

      The “url” field is not available in the Exchange Tasks calendar.

    • Type additional notes you may have about the to-do item in the “note” field.

  4. Click Close when you’re finished.

    To hide the To Do list, click the To Do button again.

When you’ve completed a task, select its checkbox in the To Do list.

You can change when to-do items are visible and when they are removed from your To Do list in the Advanced pane of kCal preferences. You can also drag to-do items to change the order in the list, or sort them using the pop-up menu at the top of the list.
