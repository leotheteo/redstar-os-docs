General preferences

Use the General pane of kCal preferences to set basic day, week, event, and alarm preferences.

Option
Description

Days per week

Choose to show 5 (Monday through Friday) or 7 (Sunday through Saturday) days per week in Week view.

Start week on

Choose the day you want the week to start on in Week and Month view. You can choose any day of the week.

Scroll in week view by

Choose whether you want to scroll by days or weeks in week view. If you choose to scroll by days, the current day will be set as the first day of the week.

Day starts at

Choose the hour when you want days to start. You can choose an hour between midnight and noon.

Day ends at

Choose the hour when you want days to end. You can choose an hour between noon and midnight.

Show

Choose how many hours you want to show in Day and Week view. You can choose to show between 6 and 24 hours at a time.

Show event times in month view

Select the checkbox to show the time of the day when events start in Month view.

Show Birthdays calendar

Select the checkbox to show the Birthdays calendar in kCal. The Birthdays calendar displays birthdays that are entered in Address Book.

Add default alarm to all new events and invitations

Select the checkbox to have an alarm added to new events and invitations. You can change when the alarm goes off by entering a new number in the “minutes before the start time” field.

Synchronize kCal with other computers and devices using MobileMe

Select the checkbox to always sync your calendars using MobileMe. If you haven’t set up your MobileMe subscription, click MobileMe.
