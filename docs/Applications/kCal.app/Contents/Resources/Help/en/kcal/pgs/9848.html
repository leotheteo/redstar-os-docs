Finding an event or to-do item

You can use the search field to quickly find events or to-do items. You can search for the name of the event or to-do item, or for the name of someone invited to the event.

To find an event or to-do item:

  • Make sure the calendars you want to search have a checkmark by them in the Calendars list.

  • To limit your search, choose an item from the magnifying glass pop-up menu (in the search field) in the top-right corner of the kCal window.

    For example, you can choose to search only the invitees in events or notes in to-do items.

  • Type a letter or word that appears in the item you’re trying to find.

    As you type, events that contain the text you entered appear in the search results list, and all other events become transparent in the main calendar view.

  • To go to the day of an event, click the event in the search results list.

    You can also click a column heading to sort the found items.

To see all the events on your calendar again and hide the search results list, delete the text from the search field (or click the “x” in the search field).
