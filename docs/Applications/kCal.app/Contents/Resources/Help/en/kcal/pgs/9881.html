Repeating an event or to-do alarm (“snoozing”)

After an alarm appears for an event or to-do item, you can dismiss it and have kCal remind you again later; this is also called setting the alarm to “snooze.”

To set the alarm to snooze:

When you see or hear the alarm, click the button with the curved arrow (near the upper-right corner), and then from the pop-up menu, choose how long you want the alarm to snooze.
