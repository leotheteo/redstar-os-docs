kCal symbols

Symbols next to calendars, events, to-do items, and invitees provide information about the items.

symbol description

The events on the calendar (or on all the calendars in a calendar group) are showing in the main calendar view. To hide the events, deselect the checkbox.

The to-do item has been completed

The event or to-do item occures regularly.

The event or to-do item has one or more alarms.

Shows the priority assigned to the to-do item: one bar means “low,” two bars mean “medium,” and three bars mean “high.”
