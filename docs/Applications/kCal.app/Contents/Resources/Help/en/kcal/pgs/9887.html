Organizing your to-do items

You can drag to-do items to reorder them, or you can sort them by due date, calendar, priority, or name.

To arrange the items in your To Do list:

  • Drag the items into the order you want.

  • To automatically sort your to-do items by due date, calendar, priority, or name, choose an item from the pop-up menu at the top of the list.
