kCal overview

kCal is a personal calendar application that lets you easily keep track of appointments, view multiple calendars at the same time, share your calendar information with others, and more. Here are some of the things you can do using kCal:

Create multiple calendars

Create calendars to track activities separately for different members of your household or different kinds of events. For example, you can create one calendar showing your work meetings and another calendar showing your child’s soccer practice. This makes it easy to focus on the information you need and to share only the calendar information you want to share.

Create events or to-do items on a calendar

Create personal appointments, meetings, or any other activity. You can also set how often you want an event to occur, set a due date for a to-do item, choose to have an alarm notify you before an event or to-do item occurs, or create an all-day event.
