Going to a specific date in the calendar

You can quickly view other parts of your calendar using the navigation buttons in the kCal window.

To jump to a specific date:

  • To go to the next or previous day, week, or month, use the Forward or Back button at the top of the kCal window.

  • Choose View > “Go to Date,” type the date you want to jump to, and then click Show.

  • To go to a specific date, click that date in the mini calendar in the lower-left corner of the kCal window. If you don’t see a mini calendar, click the calendar button in the bottom-left corner of the kCal window.

  • To see the next or previous month in the mini calendar, click the left or right arrow above the mini calendar.

  • To go to today’s date, click the Today button in the upper-left corner of the kCal window.

  • To switch to Day view from Month view, double-click the number of the day in Month view to go to that day. To switch from Week view to Day view, double-click the date at the top of the main calendar view.
