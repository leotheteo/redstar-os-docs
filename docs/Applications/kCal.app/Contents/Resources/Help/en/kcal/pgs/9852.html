Creating a new calendar

You can create separate calendars for different areas of your life (work, home, school, and so on), or for each person who shares your computer and uses the same login name and password that you use.

To create a new calendar:

  1. Click the Add (+) button at the bottom of the kCal window.

  2. Type a name for your new calendar and press the Return key.

After you create your calendars, you can add events to them, group similar calendars together, change calendar colors, and more.
