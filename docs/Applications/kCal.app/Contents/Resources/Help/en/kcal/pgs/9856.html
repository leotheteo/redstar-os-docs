If you don’t see your events or to-do items

If you don’t see an event or to-do item on your calendar or are having trouble finding it, there are several possible causes.

Try these suggestions:

  • Make sure the calendar that contains the event or to-do item has a checkmark by its name in the Calendars list. (To see whether a calendar in a calendar group has a checkmark, click the disclosure triangle next to the calendar group to open it.) You can see events and to-do items only on checkmarked calendars.

  • If you see a scroll bar on the right side of the kCal window, drag it up or down to see times that aren’t visible on the calendar.

  • Enter the name (or portion of the name) of the event or to-do item in the search field in the upper-right corner of the window.

  • An event that spans more than one day appears only on the day it starts in Month view. To see the whole event, switch to Week view by clicking the Week button at the top of the kCal window. Or to make the event an all-day event, double-click the event. If necessary, click Edit to open the event editor. Select the “all-day” checkbox.

  • If you don’t see your all-day events, make sure Show All-Day Events has a checkmark beside it in the View menu.

  • If you’ve changed the time zone for a specific event or have changed the kCal time zone (and are viewing all your events in a time zone different from your computer’s time zone), make sure you’re looking for the event at the correct time. kCal adjusts where the event appears based on the kCal time zone and the event’s time zone. To see the kCal time zone, look in the upper-right corner of the kCal window. (If you don’t see a time zone pop-up menu, kCal is displaying your events based on your computer’s time zone.)

  • Make sure that your computer’s date, time, and time zone are set correctly in Date & Time preferences. (Choose Apple menu > System Preferences and click Date & Time.)
