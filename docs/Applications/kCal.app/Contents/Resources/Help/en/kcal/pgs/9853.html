Viewing calendars

You can view more than one calendar at the same time. You can also choose not to view certain calendars.

To view calendars:

  • To see the events on a calendar, select the calendar’s checkbox in the Calendars list.

  • To hide the events on a calendar, deselect its checkbox.

  • To show or hide the events on all the calendars in the list, hold down the Command (⌘) key while you click any checkbox in the Calendars list.

  • To show or hide the calendars in a group, click the disclosure triangle next to the calendar group in the Calendars list.
