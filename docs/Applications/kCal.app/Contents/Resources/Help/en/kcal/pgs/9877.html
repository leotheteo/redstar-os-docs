Changing a calendar’s color

You can change the color of any calendar in your Calendars list. (Changing a calendar’s color also changes the color of its events.)

To change the color of a calendar:

  1. Select the calendar in the Calendars list and choose Edit > Get Info.

  2. Choose a new color from the pop-up menu in the top-right corner of the Info window.

    To customize the color, choose Other, and then make your color selections in the Colors window.
