Changing a to-do item

You can change any information about a to-do item, or put a checkmark next to a to-do item to indicate it’s completed.

You can’t change the to-do items associated with a calendar you’ve subscribed to.

To work with to-do items:

  • To show the list of to-do items, click the To Do button in the lower-right corner of the kCal window (looks like a pushpin).

  • To edit a to-do item, double-click it in the To Do list, and then make your changes in the editor. You can choose various settings for a to-do item when you create it.

  • To mark a to-do item as completed, select the checkbox (or alert icon, if the event is past its due date) next to the item in the To Do list.

  • To delete a to-do item, select it in the To Do list, and then press the Delete key.

  • To automatically delete to-do items after they’re completed, choose kCal > Preferences and click Advanced. Select the “Delete to do items” checkbox, and then enter the number of days after they’re completed that you want them deleted.

  • To manually change the order of the items in your To Do list, drag an item to a new position in the list.

  • To sort the To Do list by date, priority, or other attribute, choose a sort order from the pop-up menu at the top of the list.
