Choosing the days and times that appear on the calendar

You can view events by day, week, or month. You can also change what days and times appear in the main calendar view.

To customize the calendar view:

  • To view the events for the current day, week, or month, click the Day, Week, or Month button at the top of the kCal window.

  • To see events that are earlier or later in the day, use the scroll bar on the right side of the main calendar view.

  • To show the mini calendar, click the calendar button in the lower-left corner of the kCal window.

    To show more than one month in the mini calendar, drag the top border of the mini calendar upward. To change which months are shown, click the arrows at the top of the mini calendar.

  • To change the days of the week that appear in the main calendar view, choose kCal > Preferences and click General.

    To show only weekdays in the weekly calendar view, choose 5 from the “Days per week” pop-up menu; choose 7 to show all the days of the week. If you choose to show all the days of the week, you can make your weekly calendar begin on a specific day by choosing it from the “Start week on” pop-up menu.

  • To change the time range that appears in the main calendar view, choose kCal > Preferences and click General.

    To change the range of time when you’re normally available each day, choose your daytime hours from the “Day starts at” and “Day ends at” pop-up menus. All times outside that range are shaded in the daily or weekly calendar view. To set the number of hours that are displayed when you open the daily or weekly calendar view, choose a number from the Show pop-up menu.

You can also use keyboard shortcuts to quickly navigate within the main calendar view.
