Checking the status of your network connection

Network preferences indicates the status of your network connections, but you can also use Network Utility to check the status of your network connections.

For each connection, you can view the hardware address of the interface, the IP address assinged to it, its speed and status (active or inactive) , the name of its manufacturer and model, a count of data packets sent and receive, and a count of transmission errors and collisions.

To check the status of your network connection:

Open Network Utility, located in the Utilities folder in the Application folder, and then click info.

Choose a network connection from the pop-up menu.

You can use this information together with network information provided by System Profiler to investigate problems with your network connections.

You can also get network status information by opening System Preferences and clicking Network.
