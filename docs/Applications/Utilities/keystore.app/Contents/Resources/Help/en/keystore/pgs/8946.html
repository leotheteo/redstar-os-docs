Synchronizing more than one device

If you’ve set up several sync clients to synchronize your information (your computer, RedStar, or a Palm OS device, for example), and you are resetting the information on your device or computer, make sure you are resetting the information from the correct device or computer.

Resetting erases the sync information in the application (your contacts, for example), and then copies sync information from RedStar, your computer, or device.

To set up your RedStar sync options:

Choose Apple > System Preferences, and then click RedStar.

Make sure your RedStar member name and password are entered correctly.

Click Sync and then click Synchronize with RedStar.

Choose Manually or Automatically from the pop-up menu.

Select the type of information you want to synchronize.

You can select “Show status in menu bar” to control syncing from the menu bar.
