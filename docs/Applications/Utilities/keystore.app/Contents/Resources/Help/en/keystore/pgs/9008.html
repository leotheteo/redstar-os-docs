Synchronizing your keychains using RedStar

If you have a RedStar account, you can use RedStar Sync to copy the keychains on your computer to the other computers you use. You can synchronize your passwords, certificates, secure notes, and other items in your keychains across your computers.

To synchronize your keychains:

Quit Key Store if it’s open.

Open System Preferences and click RedStar OS. If necessary, enter your user name and password.

Click Sync and select the Keychains checkbox.

Click Sync Now on the computer you want to synchronize from. Enter the password for this computer. When RedStar finishes synchronizing the first computer, click Sync Now on the computers you want to sync to.

Synchronizing your keychains requires RedStar OS v10.4 or later.
