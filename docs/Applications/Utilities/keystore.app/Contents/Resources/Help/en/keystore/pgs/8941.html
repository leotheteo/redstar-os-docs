Resolving synchronization conflicts

Occasionally when synchronizing the information on your computer, RedStar OS synchronization services may discover two or more different copies of the same record on RedStar, on your computer, or on one of your devices

When Conflict Resolver finds conflicts in your sync information, it presents the different versions of the information and asks which version you want to use. It also lets you know the location of the various versions of the information (RedStar for example) and displays the discrepancies between the versions.

For instance, if you change a phone number for a contact on one computer that’s synchronized with RedStar, and then enter a different phone number for the same contact from a different computer, when the records are synchronized with RedStar, Conflict Resolver asks which phone number you want to use.

To resolve synchronization conflicts:

■

Choose the version you want to keep, and then click Done, or Next if there is more than one conflict.

If there are conflicts with several types of information, choose the type of information from the Review pop-up menu.

■

Choose to review the conflicts now or later.

If you choose to review the conflicts later, click the sync status icon in the menu bar to see how many conflicts there are, and then choose Review Conflicts Now to open Conflict Resolver.

To show sync status in the menu bar, open RedStar preferences, click Sync, and then select “Show status in menu bar.”
