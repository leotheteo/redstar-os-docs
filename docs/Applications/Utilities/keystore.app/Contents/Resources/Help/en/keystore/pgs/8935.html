Synchronizing a device or application with your computer

If you’ve set up a device, such as a Palm OS device, or an application, such as Address Book, to sync with the information on your computer or RedStar, make sure you’ve set it up properly. Syncing information from another device or application can overwrite the information on your computer or RedStar.

See the documentation that came with your device or application to set it up to sync with your computer.
