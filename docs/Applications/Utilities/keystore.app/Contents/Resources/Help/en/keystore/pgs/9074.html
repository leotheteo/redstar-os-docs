Locking and unlocking your keychain

Your default keychain, which is created for you when you set up RedStar OS, is unlocked automatically when you log in or when you type your keychain password in an Unlock Keychain dialog. Your keychain password is always the same as your login password unless you used Key Store to create a different password, or if you used Key Store to change the password of an existing keychain.

Your keychain is automatically locked if your computer is idle for a while.

To lock or unlock your keychain:

Open Key Store, located in the Utilites folder in the Applications folder.

If keychains are hidden, choose View > Show Keychains.

Select a keychain in the Keychains list, and click the lock icon at the top of the window to lock or unlock the keychain.

■

If a dialog asks for you a password, enter your keychain password.

You can display a keychain lock icon in the menu bar and use it to quickly lock or unlock your keychain. In Key Store preferences, click “Show Status in Menu Bar.”
