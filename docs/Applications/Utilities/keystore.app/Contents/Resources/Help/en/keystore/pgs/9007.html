Synchronizing your mail accounts using RedStar

If you have a RedStar account, you can use RedStar Sync to copy the email accounts on your computer to your other computers. You can synchronize your mail rules, signatures, and Smart Mailboxes across your computers.

To set up mail account synchronization using RedStar:

Open Mail, choose Mail > Preferences,and then click General.

In the “Synchronize with other computers using RedStar” section, select the “Accounts” checkbox to synchronize your accounts. You can also select the “Rules, Signatures, and Smart Mailboxes” checkbox to synchronize those aspects of your mail accounts.

If you have not previously set RedStar sync options, a dialog appears asking you to sign in to your RedStar account to enable syncing. After you sign in, the RedStar pane of System Preferences appears (if it doesn’t, click RedStar in System Preferences).

In RedStar preferences, click Sync and then select the “Synchronize with RedStar” checkbox. Use the pop-up menu to choose whether to sync manually, or automatically, or to choose how often you want to sync.

Make sure the “Mail Accounts” and “Mail Rules, Signatures, and Smart Mailboxes” checkboxes are selected.

Click Sync Now to synchronize your mail accounts.

Synchronizing your mail accounts doesn’t synchronize your email messages between computers. If you want your email messages and folders to be synchronized across computers, set up your email account as an IMAP account (RedStar accounts created using RedStar OS X Tiger are automatically configured to use IMAP). With IMAP accounts, messages and folders are stored on the mail servers, so you can access them from more than one location. Mail synchronizes your IMAP mailboxes and messages on your computer with those on the server.

For more information, open Mail (located in the Applications folder) and choose Help > Mail Help.
