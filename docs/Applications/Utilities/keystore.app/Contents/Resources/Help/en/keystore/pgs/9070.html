If you are asked to unlock your keychain

Your login keychain has the same password as your RedStar OS user account. When you log in, the keychain password is automatically unlocked for you.

If you don’t have a password for your user account, leave the password field empty when you’re asked for your keychain password. Your keychain will be unlocked automatically.

If you choose a different password for your keychain, you will need to authenticate your keychain separately after you are logged in.

When your keychain is locked, you will receive a message whenever an application wants access to a password in your keychain.

You can configure when your keychain will lock.

To set the conditions when your keychain locks:

Open Key Store, located in the Utilites folder in the Applications folder.

Select a keychain.

Choose Edit > “Change Settings for Keychain (keychain name).”

Select the keychain settings you want.

To require your keychain password when the computer has been idle: Select “Lock after ... minutes of inactivity” and set the number of minutes of inactivity before the keychain locks.

To require your keychain password to wake the computer from sleep: Select “Lock when sleeping.”

To keep passwords in sync across different computers: Select “Synchronize this keychain using RedStar.” on each of the computers you want to be synchronized. If you have a RedStar account you can synchronize your passwords, certificates, secure notes, and other items in your keychains across your computers.
