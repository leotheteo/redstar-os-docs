Setting up the Data Change Alert

When you synchronize your computers using RedStar, or your devices using iSync, the Data Change Alert displays the changes that will be made, and lets you confirm whether you want to change the information. The Data Change Alert informs you what information will be added, deleted, or modified on your computer or device.

You can cancel or proceed with the synchronization at this point. If you’re sure you want to continue with the synchronization, click Allow. If you’re not sure, you can click Cancel, and the entire synchronization is canceled.

Use iSync preferences to set up the Data Change Alert.

To set up the Data Change Alert:

Open iSync (located in the Applications folder), and choose iSync > Preferences.

Select the “Show Data Change Alert when” checkbox, and choose an option from the pop-up menu.

■

Choose “any” if you want the Data Change Alert to inform you when any amount of information will change.

■

Choose “more than 5%,” “more than 25%,” or “more than 50%” if you want the Data Change Alert to inform you when the percentage of information you chose will change.

You may see more than one change alert window depending on what information is being synchronized, and what the current alert settings are in iSync Preferences. Click Allow, to change only the information type displayed in the each Data Change Alert window. Click Cancel to cancel the entire sync.

To prevent the Data Change Alert from appearing, deselect the “Show Data Change Alert when” checkbox in iSync preferences.
