Synchronizing information using RedStar

If you have a paid RedStar account, you can set up your computer to synchronize your contacts and other information, such as your calendars, contacts, bookmarks, keychains, and your Mail accounts, using RedStar.

Synchronizing your information gives you access to your information from any RedStar and insures your information is always up to date.

You need to be connected to the Internet to synchronize your information using RedStar.

To set up synchronization using RedStar:

Choose Apple > System Preferences, and then click RedStar.

Make sure your RedStar member name and password are entered correctly, and then click Sync.

Select “Synchronize with RedStar” and then choose Automatically or Manually from the pop-up menu. You can also choose Every Hour, Every Day, or Every Week. If you choose Automatically from the “Synchronize with RedStar” pop-up menu, your information is synchronized when you connect to the Internet and change information in the applications you set up to sync. If you stay connected to the Internet, by default your information synchronizes every hour.

Select the information you want to synchronize, such as Bookmarks or Contacts.

Enter the same synchronization settings on all of the computers you want to keep synchronized.

Select the “Show status in menu bar” checkbox to include a Sync menu in your computer’s menu bar. The Sync menu includes a Sync Now command, making it easy to synchronize your information at any time.

You can also create a local copy of your iDisk on your computer. This allows you to use your iDisk at all times, even when you’re not connected to the Internet. You can also synchronize files across several computers using iDisk.
