Resetting synchronized information

Once you’ve set up your computer to sync with your .RedStar account, you can reset the information you want to synchronize anytime you are connected to the Internet. Use the Sync pane of RedStar preferences to set up the type of information you want to synchronize, for example, your contacts or calendars.

If you’re not sure you want to keep the changes made during your last synchronization, you can reset the information you want to synchronize on your computer to match the information on RedStar, or you can reset the information on RedStar with the information on your computer.

You may also want to reset your information if you’ve had problems shnchronizing and want to “start over.”

You need to be connected to the Internet to synchronize with RedStar.

To reset sync information:

Choose Apple > System Preferences, and then click RedStar.

Click Advanced, select the computer you want to use, and then click Reset Sync Data.

Choose the sync information you want to reset, such as Bookmarks, Keychains, or all data from the Replace pop-up menu.

Click the arrow pointing toward the computer icon to copy the information from your RedStar account to your computer. This replaces the information on your computer with the information on RedStar. Click the arrow pointing toward the RedStar icon to copy the information on your computer to your RedStar account. This replaces the information on RedStar with the information on your computer.

Using the Reset Sync Data command erases the information on your devices and replaces it with the information from the source you selected.
