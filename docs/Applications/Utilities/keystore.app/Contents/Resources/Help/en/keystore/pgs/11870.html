Changing the default keychain for your account

Your default keychain is the keychain that’s being used when you log in to your account. When the user creates passwords for servers or applications, the passwords are added to this keychain. When an account is created, the default keychain for that account is called “login.”

To make a keychain your default keychain:

Open Key Store, located in the Utilites folder in the Applications folder.

If the Keychains list is hidden, choose View > Show Keychains.

Select your new keychain from the list, and choose File > Make Keychain (keychain name) Default.

If you want your keychain to be unlocked when you log in to your account, your default keychain and your account must have the same password.
