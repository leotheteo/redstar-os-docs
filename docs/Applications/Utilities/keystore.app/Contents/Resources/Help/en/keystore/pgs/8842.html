About digital identity

A digital identity is an electronic means of identifying someone. It consists of a certificate containing a key you publish freely (your “public key”), and a secret key you keep private (your “private key”).

Your private key allows you to sign electronic documents whose signatures everyone can verify using only your public key. Likewise, your private key can reveal (decrypt) documents that were concealed (encrypted) by others using your public key.

For a digital identity to be accepted as an item of integrity, it needs to be trusted by the recipient. To help ensure the authenticity of a digital identity, certification authorities supply digital identities to individuals whose identities have been verified.
