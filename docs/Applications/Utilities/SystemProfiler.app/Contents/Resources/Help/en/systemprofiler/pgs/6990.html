Getting information about your hardware

You can get detailed information about the hardware that is installed in or connected to your computer. System Profiler shows information about drives, video cards, memory, USB devices, and much more.

To get information about your hardware:

Click Hardware in the Contents list.

You see general information such as the computer’s serial number and the type of processor.

To see more detailed hardware information, click the disclosure triangle beside Hardware, and then click a subcategory to see all the information about it.
