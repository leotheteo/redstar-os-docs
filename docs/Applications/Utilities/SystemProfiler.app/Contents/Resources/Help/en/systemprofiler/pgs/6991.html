Getting information about your network

You can view complete details about your computer’s network connections, including information about your active services, Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, locations, and the type and speed of your network connections.

To get information about your active network connections:

Click Network in the Contents list.

In the Active Services list, click the connection you want information about.

For other network information, click the disclosure triangle to display the network subcategories, then select a subcategory for details.
