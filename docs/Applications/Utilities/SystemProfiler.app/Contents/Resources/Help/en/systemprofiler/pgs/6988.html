Getting information about your software

You can get detailed information about the software on your computer, including information on the operating system, applications, and much more. System Profiler shows the versions, modification dates, and locations of each software item.

To get information about your software:

Click Software in the Contents list.

You see general information such as the operating system version, the boot volume, and the computer name.

To see more detailed information available, click the disclosure triangle beside “Software,” click a subcategory to see its items, and then click an item to see all the information on it.

For example, to see details about Simple Text, click the disclosure triangle beside Software, click Applications, and then click Simple Text.
