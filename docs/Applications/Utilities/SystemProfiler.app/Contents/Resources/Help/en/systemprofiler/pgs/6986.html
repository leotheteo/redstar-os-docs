If you can’t find the information you expected

If you can’t find some information you expected to see in your computer’s profile, you may need to view a full profile or refresh the profile.

If you can’t find the information you expected, try the following:

■

Choose View > Full Profile to see all the information available.

You may have been viewing a mini profile or basic profile.

■

If you’ve changed settings or disconnected peripherals since you opened System Profiler, choose View > Refresh to update the information in the profile.

Some peripheral devices are not always active even when physically connected to your computer. For example, some devices may only be active during syncing. These devices are displayed in your computer’s profile only if they’re active.
