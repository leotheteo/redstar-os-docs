Getting information about processes

A process is a program running on your computer. It can be an application that is visible on your screen or one of many invisible background processes running in the background that provide functions your computer needs.

Programmers and system administrators may need to check the background processes running on a computer to monitor performance or to diagnose problems.

You can use Activity Monitor to view all the processes running on your system and to see detailed information about them, such as who the user is, the percentage of processor time being used, the number of threads or tasks a process is running, or the amount of memory being used by each.

Here are ways to get information on processes:

■

To filter the processes shown, you can choose a type of process from the Show pop-up menu. You can also filter processes by typing part of the process name in the Filter field.

■

To see more information about a process, select it click Inspect.

■

To sort the processes, click the column headings. Click the triangle in the selected column heading to reverse the order.

■

To see general information, such as the number of processes and threads open on your computer, click CPU and view the information at the bottom of the pane.
