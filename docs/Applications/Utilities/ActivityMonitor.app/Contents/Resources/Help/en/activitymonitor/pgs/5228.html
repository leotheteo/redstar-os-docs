Checking disk space

You can use Activity Monitor to see how the hard disk space is being used and how much space is available. The space utilized and space free are displayed in a pie chart in bytes.

To check the hard drive space:

Click Disk Usage in the button bar.

Choose the name of the hard disk you want to examine from the pop-up menu.

The total hard disk space used and space free is displayed in bytes.

You can change the colors used in the chart by clicking the color box next to each item.
