Viewing network activity on your system

You can use Activity Monitor to display the amount of information your system is sending and receiving through the network. Your system transfers information in small chunks called packets. Activity Monitor shows the number of packets received by your system and the number of packets sent out. The speed of information being transferred is shown as the rate of packets in seconds. The total amount of information moved is shown in megabytes. The amount of information moved over time, or throughput, is shown in a graph.

To see the amount of network activity:

Click Network in the button bar.

You can select the type of network activity displayed in the graph. Click on Packets or Data. You can change the color of items in the graph by clicking the color box next to each item.
