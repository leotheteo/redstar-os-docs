Grouping processes for easier viewing

Activity Monitor displays all the processes running on your computer. You might be surprised at how many processes are running on your computer even when you see only one application open. An application may have multiple processes that run in the background. Many of these background processes are not visible.

Other processes are used by the operating system to perform functions on your computer.

To view processes by group:

Choose one of the following process types from the Show pop-up menu.

All Processes: Shows all the processes running on the system.

All Processes, Hierarchically: Shows all processes which belong to other processes. The processes are displayed hierarchically.

My Processes: Shows processes owned by your user account.

Administrator Processes: Shows processes owned by users with administrative privileges.

Other User Processes: Shows processes that aren’t owned by the root or the current user.

Active Processes: Shows processes that are running and not sleeping.

Inactive Processes: Shows processes that are running but asleep.

Windowed Processes: Shows processes that create a window. Not all windows may be visible to the user.

Selected Processes: Shows only the processes selected when this item is chosen.
