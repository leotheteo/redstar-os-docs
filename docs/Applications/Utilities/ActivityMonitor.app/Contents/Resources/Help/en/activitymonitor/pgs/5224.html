Viewing disk activity

Excessive disk access can slow down the performance of your system. Disk fragmentation or inadequate memory may require your computer to retrieve information from the hard disk too frequently. This requires that the computer wait for information to be retrieved before performing tasks.

You can use Activity Monitor to display the amount of activity on your computer’s hard disks. Activity Monitor tracks the number of times the hard drive is accessed for reading and writing to your hard disks. It also shows the amount of data that’s read from and written to your disks. The amount of data moved over time, called performance or throughput, is displayed on a line graph.

To see the amount of disk activity:

Click Disk Activity in the button bar.

You can change the color of items in the chart by clicking the color box next to each item.
