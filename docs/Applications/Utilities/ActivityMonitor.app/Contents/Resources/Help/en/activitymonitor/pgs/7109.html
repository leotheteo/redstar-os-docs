About Activity Monitor

Activity Monitor can display information about processes running on your computer, the processor, disk, memory, and network. You can set up floating windows that show your CPU usage, or even set up the Activity Monitor Dock icon to display this activity.

See the processes running on your system
Activity Monitor can display a complete list of the processes running on your computer. You can view the processes organized in different groupings, export a list of processes, or use Activity Monitor to quit a process. You can use the search field to find the processes you are looking for.

Monitoring the processor activity
You can use the Activity Monitor utility to observe the changing workload on your processor or CPU (central processing unit). You can set up small floating windows that display the amount of processor usage, or history. You can view the graphs as you perform tasks and see which actions are making the greatest demands of your processor.

Memory, disk, and network usage
Activity Monitor can show you information about how your computer’s memory is being used, and how much memory space is available. You can monitor the amount of disk activity on your computer or check the amount of space currently available on your hard disk. Activity Monitor can also display the amount of data being transferred in and out of the network by your computer.
