Seeing detailed information about a disk or volume

You can see detailed information about a disk or volume on your computer.

Depending on the type of disk or volume, the information includes its format, its total capacity, the amount of space available, and its mount point.

To see detailed information about a disk or volume:

Select a disk or volume in the list, and then click Info in the toolbar.

A summary of the detailed information about the selected disk or volume appears at the bottom of the Disk Utility window.

You can also use System Profiler to see information about your system’s hardware and software.
