Erasing a hard disk or volume

You can use Disk Utility to erase hard disks and volumes. Erasing a hard disk deletes all its volumes and files. Erasing a volume deletes all the files on that volume, but does not affect other volumes.

Notice: If you have any files you want to save, be sure to copy them to another disk before you erase the disk they are on.

To erase a disk or volume:

If you want to erase an external disk, connect the disk to your computer.

In Disk Utility, select the disk or volume in the list on the left.

Click Erase.

Choose a format from the Volume Format pop-up menu.

Type a name for the disk or volume.

Click Erase.

In Erase Option, select the option you want, and click OK.

Click Erase.
