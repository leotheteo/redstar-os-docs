Putting your computer to sleep automatically

You can put your computer to sleep automatically when it’s inactive for a period of time. You can set the entire computer to sleep or just the display. If your computer is in the middle of a task that you want to finish, you should set only the display to sleep.

To put your computer to sleep automatically:

Choose RedStar menu > System Preferences, and click Energy Saver.

Select options you want to use.

■

If you’re using a portable Mac, choose Power Adapter from the “Settings for” pop-up menu to specify the settings to use when your computer is connected to the power adapter. Choose Battery to specify the settings to use when your computer is using its battery. Choose an option appropriate for your use of the computer from the Optimization pop-up menu or choose Custom to specify your settings.

■

If you’re using a desktop Mac or specifying custom settings for a portable Mac, drag the sliders that control sleep for the computer and display.

To put the computer’s hard disk to sleep whenever it’s inactive, select the checkbox labeled “Put the hard disk(s) to sleep when possible.”

To set a daily schedule for putting your computer to sleep or turning it off and on, click Schedule and select the options you want to use.
