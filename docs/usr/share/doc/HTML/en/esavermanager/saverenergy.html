Energy Saver

You can save energy by putting your computer to sleep when you aren't using it. You set sleep preferences in the Energy Saver preferences.

To save energy with your computer,

■

If you're using a desktop computer, open the Energy preferences, and drag the sliders to set a time for your computer to go to sleep.

■

Click Schedule in Energy Saver preferences to schedule a time for your computer to shut down to make sure it's turned off when you're not working.

■

Don't use a screen saver; instead,put your display to sleep.
