  Discover System Preferences

You use System Preferences to customize various aspects of your computer, such as what your desktop looks like, how you interact with your computer, or how you share information with other computer users.

When you open System Preferences the first time, individual preferences are grouped in the categories shown below. Click a link to find out more about settings and preferences in each category.

Personal
Use these preferences to customize the overall look of System, the language used for menus and dialogs, and security settings for your computer.
■
Personal preferences
Hardware
Choose settings to control devices connected to your computer, such as displays, keyboards, and printers. Adjust settings for sound and energy conservation, and determine what happens when you insert a CD or DVD disc.
■
Hardware preferences
Internet & Network
Use these preferences to configure Internet access, network settings, shared disks and folders settings.
■
Internet & Network preferences
System
These preferences help you set up user accounts, and select your startup disk. You can also format date and time settings.
■
