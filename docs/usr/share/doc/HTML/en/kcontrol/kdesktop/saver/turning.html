  Turning screen savers on and off

You can start or deactivate your screen saver by moving the pointer to a corner of the screen. This is called setting a “hot corner.”

To set hot corners:

Choose RedStar menu -> System Preferences, click Desktop & Screen Saver, and then click Screen Saver.

Click Hot Corners.

Choose Start Screen Saver or Disable Screen Saver from the pop-up menu for a corner you want to use; then click OK.
