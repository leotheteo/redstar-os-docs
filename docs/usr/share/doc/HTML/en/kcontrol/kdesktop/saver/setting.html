  Setting up a screen saver

You can have images appear on your screen when you aren’t using your computer. You may want to do this to hide the items on your desktop while you’re away.

To set up a screen saver:

Choose RedStar menu->System Preferences, click Desktop & Screen Saver, and then click Screen Saver.

Choose a screen saver from the list.

For more information about the options, click the Help (question mark) button.
