  Changing the screen background

You can change the background area of your screen to a different pattern, color, or picture.

To customize your screen background:

Choose RedStar menu -> System Preferences, and then click Desktop & Screen Saver.

Click Desktop.

Choose a folder from the list on the left; then click a specific pattern or picture in the sample box on the right.

For more information about the choices, click the Help (question mark) button.
