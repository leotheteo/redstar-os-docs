  Hiding the Restart, Sleep, and Shut Down buttons on the login window

It may be possible for some with physical access to your computer to bypass the login window by turning off your computer, then restarting it with a CD.

To make it more difficult for users to bypass logging in, you can hide the Shut Down and Restart buttons in the login window.

To hide the Restart, Sleep, and Shut Down buttons:

Choose RedStar menu > System Preferences, and click Accounts.

If some settings are dimmed, click the lock icon and type an administrator name and password.

Click Login Options.

Deselect the “Show the Restart, Sleep, and Shut Down buttons” checkbox.
