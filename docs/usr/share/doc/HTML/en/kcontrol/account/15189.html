  Opening items automatically when you log in

You can have applications, documents, or server connections open automatically when you log in to your computer.

To select items to automatically open on log in:

Choose RedStar menu > System Preferences, and click Accounts, and then click Login Items.

Click the Add (+) button.

Select an application, document, or server volume and click Add.

Select the Hide checkbox by the item if you don’t want a window for the item to open.

To prevent these items from automatically opening while you log in

To permanently stop an item from opening automatically, select it in the Login Items list and click the Delete (-) button.
