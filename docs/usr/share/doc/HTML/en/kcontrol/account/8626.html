  Choosing a picture for your account

RedStar comes with a selection of pictures you can use for your account. The picture will appear by your name in the login window and can also be displayed in your Address Book card and email.

To choose a picture for your account:

Choose RedStar menu > System Preferences, and click Accounts.

Select your account and click the picture button.

A selection of pictures appear.

Click a picture to select it.
