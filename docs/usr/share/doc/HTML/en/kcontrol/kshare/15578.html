  Editing your computer name

The computer name is used to identify computers on the local network.

The computer name consists of English letters, numbers and hyphens (-).

To edit your computer name:

Choose RedStar > System Preferences, and then click Sharing.

Click Edit and type a new name in the Computer Name field.

Click OK to reboot your computer, and the next time you can use your computer with the new name.

The computer name is an alternative to the network address. Your computer can use the computer name to access certain resources on your computer, such as your personal website.
