  Sharing your computer with others on your network

You can set up your computer to share files, folders, and other services with other users on your network. You can share your computer's screen.

Some services, such as sharing files with users on Windows computers require that you set up permissions on your computer. Use the information on this page to help you set up your computer to share the services you want, with the users you want to share with.

File sharing
You can set up your computer to share your entire computer with everyone, or restrict the sharing service to allow specific users access to only certain folders. You can share files using File Transfer Protocol (FTP) or Server Message Block (SMB) protocol.

Screen sharing
You can set up your computer so that others can share your screen. While your screen is shared, a user of another computer sees what's on your screen and can open, move, and close files and windows, open applications, and even restart your computer.

Remote login
If you allow remote login, you can use Secure Shell (SSH) to log in to your computer from another location.
