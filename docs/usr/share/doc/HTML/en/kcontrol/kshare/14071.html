  If you can't share the screen of another computer

You need to make sure you're connected to the same network as the computer you want to share.

Check the following if you can't share the screen of another computer:

■

Make sure the Screen Sharing checkbox is selected in Sharing preferences on the computer you want to share.

■

Make sure the <VNC viewers may control screen with password> checkbox is selected.

Sharing your screen with other computer users
■

Make sure the computer you want to share is not in sleep mode. You can't share the screen with a computer that is in sleep. Adjust the computer's sleep settings using Energy Saver preferences.
