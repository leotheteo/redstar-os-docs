Using Date & Time preferences

You can set your computer’s date and time manually or have them set automatically using a network time server. You must specify your time zone.

Preference

Explanation

Date & Time

Manually: Deselect “Set date & time automatically.” Click today’s date on the calendar and drag the clock's hands. You can also use the up and down arrows and type over the current date or time. Click Save when you're done.

Automatically: Select “Set date & time automatically.” Open the pop-up menu and choose the server that’s closest to you, or type the address of your network time server. If your computer isn't connected to the Internet, it will connect when you choose this option.

Time Zone

Click the map to indicate your location, and then open the pop-up menu and choose the city closest to you.

Important: You must specify your time zone whether the date and time are set manually or automatically. If you don’t, your clock may show the wrong time and the time stamp on emails and files may be incorrect.

Clock

To see a clock in the menu bar or on the desktop, select Show the date and time. Choose options for how the date and time are displayed. Your changes take effect immediately so you can see the results.

To have the computer announce the time periodically, select "Announce the time" and choose a frequency from the pop-up menu.
