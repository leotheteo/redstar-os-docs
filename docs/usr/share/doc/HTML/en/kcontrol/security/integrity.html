About the Integrity pane

With the integrity pane, you can set the behavior of the intcheckd daemon and watch the log of integrity modification.

If you need to check integrities of all system files, you should enable the item of “Checks the integrity on boot-up”. And if you are about to check integrities of those on the fly, you may use “Checks the integrity at run-time”. The configuration you set will be saved and also be useful continuously.

In the part of Logs, you can watch logs of integrity modification. When you select a day in the left box, the contents according to that day will be displayed in the right box.
