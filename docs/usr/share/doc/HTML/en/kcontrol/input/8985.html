  Changing the way your mouse works

To work more comfortably, you can change the way your mouse responds as you move it.

To adjust your mouse:

Choose RedStar Menu > System Preferences, and then click Keyboard & Mouse.

Click Mouse.

Set the sliders. As you adjust the sliders, try moving and clicking your mouse to see the effect of your changes.

■

Tracking Speed: Adjusts how fast the pointer moves when you drag the mouse.

■

Double-Click Speed: Adjusts how rapidly you must click for the computer to recognize a double-click.

■

Scrolling Speed (if your mouse has a scroll ball or wheel): Adjusts how quickly the pointer moves when you scroll. The scroll ball doesn’t scroll menus or sliders in dialog boxes.

■

Primary mouse button (if your mouse has multiple buttons): Choose the button to use for most pointing and clicking.

If you have a Mighty Mouse or other device with multiple buttons, you’ll see additional options.
