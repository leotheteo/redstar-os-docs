Using General preferences

You can change the overall look of RedStar in several ways to make your working environment more comfortable or to give your desktop a unique look.

Preference

Explanation

General and Highlight Color

Choose colors from the pop-up menus to make general system color changes.

Place scroll arrows

Select one of the "Place scroll arrows" options to change where scroll arrows appear in KFinder and other application windows. To reduce the need to move the mouse, click Together.

Click in the scroll bar to

Specify what should happen when you click in the scroll bar. “Jump to the next page” means clicking in the scroll bar moves the content page-by-page. “Jump to here” moves to the spot where you're clicking; for instance, if you click at the bottom of the scroll bar, you go to the bottom of the document.

Tip: Hold the Option key while clicking the scroll bar to momentarily switch between scrolling methods.

Minimize when double clicking a window title bar

To minimize a window to the Dock by double-clicking the title bar, select this checkbox.

Number of Recent Items

The RedStar menu has a Recent Items option. Use the pop-up menus to choose how many items should appear in that menu.

Font smoothing style

To fine-tune the way text looks on your screen, choose an option from the pop-up menu.

Depending on the type of display you have, you may notice only small or no differences between smoothing styles.

If you plan to use small font sizes in your documents, choose a font size from the "Turn off text smoothing for font sizes" pop-up menu.

When text smoothing (or "antialiasing") is on, smaller fonts can be harder to read.
