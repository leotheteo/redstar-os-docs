  Showing PPPoE status in the menu bar

You can control your PPPoE connection from an icon in the menu bar instead of opening Network preferences.

To show PPPoE status in the menu bar:

Choose global menu > System Preferences, and then click Network.

In the network connection services list:

■

Select your PPPoE service.

■

If your PPPoE service isn't in the list, click Add (+) at the bottom of the list. Choose PPPoE from the Interface pop-up menu, choose an Ethernet interface from the Ethernet pop-up menu - Ethernet if it is a wired connection, or AirPort if it's wireless - give the PPPoE service a name, and then click Create.

Enter the account information you received from your service provider, and then click Apply.

Information you need to connect to the Internet

Select “Show PPPoE status in the menu bar".

Use the PPPoE status menu to connect to your PPPoE service, display time connected, and show status while connecting.
