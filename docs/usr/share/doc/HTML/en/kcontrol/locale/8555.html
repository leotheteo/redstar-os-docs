  Choosing date, time, and number formats

You can change the format used to display the date, time, and numbers on your computer to match the conventions used in a particular geographic region. For example, the date can be displayed as day/month/year or month/day/year.

These format choices affect how dates, times, and numbers appear in KFinder windows, Mail, and other applications.

To choose international formats:

Choose RedStar Menu > System Preferences and click International.

Click Formats.

Choose the geographic region that uses the format you want to use from the Region pop-up menu.

By default, only regions for your preferred language (in the Language pane) are shown. If you don’t see the region you want, select the “Show all regions" checkbox.

The examples in the window change to show the date, time, and number formats for the selected region.

To make any additional changes, click the Customize button next to the item you want to change, and make your choices.
