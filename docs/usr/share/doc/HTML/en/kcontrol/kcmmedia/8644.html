  If the wrong application opens every time you insert a disc

In CDs & DVDs preferences, you can choose what happens each time you insert a CD or DVD into your computer.

If the wrong application opens when you insert a disc, check the following:

■

Open CDs & DVDs preferences and choose what happens when you insert a disc. You can choose to open the KFinder, open a different application, or perform some other action.

Choosing what happens when you insert a music CD.
Choosing what happens when you insert a video DVD
■

The disc you’re inserting may be a different type of disc than you had thought. For example, a CD that contains MP3 files may not be considered an audio CD if you created it in the KFinder instead of iTunes.

■

Another application, such as iTunes, may be changing the action performed when you insert a disc. Check the application’s documentation for more information.
