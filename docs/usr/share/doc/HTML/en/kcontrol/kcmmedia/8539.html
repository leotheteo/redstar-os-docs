  Choosing what happens when you insert a music CD

You can determine how you want your computer to respond when you insert a music CD in the optical drive. You can:

■

Open iTunes or iPhoto to play or view your CD

■

Open another application of your choice

■

Run a script to perform a sequence of actions

■

Ignore the disc if you don't want to do anything right away.

To choose what happens when you insert a music CD:

Open System Preferences and click CDs & DVDs.

Choose an action from the two pop-up menus for music CDs.

Some applications can start processing an inserted disc immediately. For example, iTunes can import a music CD's songs as soon as its inserted. To find out how to set up an application to start processing a disc immediately, read the application's help.
