  Choosing what happens when you insert a video DVD

You can determine how you want your computer to respond when you insert a DVD-Video disc in the optical drive. You can:

■

Open DVD Player to view your DVD

■
help:topic_list=almh10094 bookID='Mac Help' template=sty/genlist.html stylesheet=sty/genlist_style.css Other=CDs & DVDs preferences

Open another application of your choice

■

Run a script to perform a sequence of actions

■

Open Disk Utility to work with the disc

■

Ignore the disc if you don't want to do anything right away

To choose what happens when you insert a video DVD:

Open System Preferences and click CDs & DVDs.

Choose an action from the pop-up menu for video DVDs.

Close the window to save your settings.

Some applications can start processing an inserted disc immediately. For example, DVD Player can play a video DVD as soon as it's inserted. To find out how to set up an application to start processing a disc immediately, read the application's help.
