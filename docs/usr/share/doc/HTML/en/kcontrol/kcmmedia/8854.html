  Choosing actions for blank discs

When you insert a blank CD or DVD, a dialog may appear asking what you want to do with the disc.

Here's how to use the dialog's options:

■

From the pop-up menu, choose an action, such as Open iTunes, that designates what should be done with the disc.

■

Select “Make this action the default” to have the computer automatically perform the same action whenever a blank disc is inserted.

■

To continue without doing anything, click Ignore.

■

To cancel all actions and remove the disc, click Eject.

You can also use CDs & DVDs preferences to set how your computer responds to blank discs.
