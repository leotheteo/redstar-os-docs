Personal preferences

Use these preferences to customize the overall look of Mac OS X, the language used for menus and dialogs, and security settings for your computer.

Appearance
Change the overall look of buttons or highlighted items, or where scroll bars appear in KFinder windows.

Change or customize the desktop background, or choose a visual screen effect to hide the items on your desktop or prevent other users from using your computer while you're away.

Dock
Set where the Dock appears on the screen, adjust the size of Dock icons, turn on magnification, or hide the Dock.
■
Dock preferences
Exposé & Spaces
Select the shortcuts you want to use to see all your windows at one time using Exposé, or set up and use your desktop spaces.

Security
Protect your computer and the information stored on it. You can disable automatic login, prevent other users from changing some of your computer's preferences, require a password to wake the computer, and automatically log users out after a period of inactivity. You can also adjust firewall settings.
