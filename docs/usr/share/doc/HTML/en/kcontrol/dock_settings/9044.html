Customizing the Dock

You can change the size of icons in the Dock for better visibility, and reposition or hide the Dock to keep it out of the way.

To customize the Dock:

Choose RedStar Menu > System Preferences, and then click Dock.

Open Dock preferences

Adjust the Dock options.

If you want to see the effect of any changes you make, be sure to deselect “Automatically hide and show the Dock.”

For more information about the Dock options, click the Help (question mark) button.

You can also choose RedStar Menu > Dock to change the way the Dock works.

To add an item to the Dock, drag its icon from a KFinder window or the desktop to the Dock. Notice that there is a separator in the Dock between application icons and the Trash. Application icons go to the left of the separator, and document icons go to the right of the separator.
