  Changing the sound balance

If your computer has two internal or external stereo speakers, you can choose to hear the sound played equally through both speakers, or shift the balance so that more sound plays through the left or right side. You can also change the balance if you’re using stereo headphones.

To change the sound balance:

Choose RedStar menu > System Preferences and click Sound.

Click Output.

Select the sound output device you’re using.

Drag the Balance slider left to hear the sound more through the left speaker, or right to shift the sound to the right speaker.
