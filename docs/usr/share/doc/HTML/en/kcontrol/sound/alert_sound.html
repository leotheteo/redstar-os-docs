  Changing the alert sound

When you try to do something that isn’t allowed in Mac OS X, or if the computer needs your input or more information, an alert dialog appears on your screen and an alert sound plays. You can change the alert sound.

To change the alert sound:

Choose RedStar menu > System Preferences and click Sound.

Click Sound Effects.

Select an alert sound in the list.

You can also choose to hear sounds when you do certain actions in the KFinder, by selecting the “Play user interface sound effects” checkbox in the Sound Effects pane of Sound preferences. If this checkbox is selected, for example, you hear a sound effect when you drag an item to the Trash.
