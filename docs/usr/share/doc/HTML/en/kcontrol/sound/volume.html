  Adjusting sound volume

You can change the volume of all sounds playing through your computer’s speakers. You can also select a separate volume for alert sounds (sounds your computer makes when alert dialogs appear). And you can mute your computer’s sound.

To adjust your computer’s sound volume preferences:

Choose RedStar menu > System Preferences and click Sound.

To increase or decrease the volume of all sounds played through your computer’s speakers (or through the output device selected in the Output pane), drag the “Output volume” slider at the bottom of the window left or right. Or click the Mute checkbox to turn off the speaker sound.

To increase or decrease the volume of alert sounds, drag the “Alert volume” slider in the Sound Effects pane right or left.

The alert volume is always relative to the main output volume; it can never be higher than the main output volume.

To control the volume of your computer from the menu bar, select the “Show volume in menu bar” checkbox.

If you have a Macintosh computer with an infrared (IR) receiver, you can adjust the sound volume remotely using the Volume Up and Volume Down buttons on an Apple Remote.

Depending on your keyboard, you may be able to change the volume using volume keys (the ones with a speaker icon) on your keyboard.

Depending on your computer and the devices you use, you may be able to set other volume options using the Audio MIDI Setup application (in the Utilities folder).

If you use applications that have volume controls (for example, iTunes or iMovie), their settings can only be equal to or less than the computer’s output volume. They do not override the settings you choose in Sound preferences.
