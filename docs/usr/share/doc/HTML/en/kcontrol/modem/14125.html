  Setting advanced modem options

If your service provider gave you settings other than the phone number, account name, and password for your dial-up PPP account, you can use the advanced panes of the modem settings in Network preferences to enter the settings.

Advanced settings include additional modem and PPP settings, as well as DNS, proxy, and WINS server settings.

To set advanced modem and PPP settings:

Choose RedStar > System Preferences, and then click Network.

Choose your modem port (internal or external) from the network connection services list, and click Advanced.

Click Modem and set the options that meet the requirements of your region and service provider.
