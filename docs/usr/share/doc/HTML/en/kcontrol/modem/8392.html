  Showing modem status in the menu bar

You can control your modem connection using an icon in the menu bar instead of opening Network preferences.

Choose RedStar > System Preferences, and then click Network.

Choose your modem service, such as Internal Modem or External Modem, from the network connection services list.

If your modem isn’t in the list, click Add (+) at the bottom of the list, choose Modem from the Interface pop-up menu, give the modem service a name, and then click Create. Enter the settings your received from your service provider, and then click Apply.

Select “Show modem status in menu bar.”

To use your modem to establish a network connection, click the modem status icon in the menu bar and choose Connect.
