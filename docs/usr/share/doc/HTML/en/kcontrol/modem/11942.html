  Setting advanced PPP options

You can control when your modem connects and disconnects from the Internet, how long your modem stays connected when it is inactive, how many time the modem will redial your service provider if the line is busy, and other PPP options.

To set PPP options:

Choose global menu > System Preferences, and then click Network.

In the network connection services list:

■

Select your modem (either internal or external, depending on what is connected to your computer).

■

If your modem isn't in the list, click Add (+) at the bottom of the list, and then choose Modem (internal or external) from the Interface pop-up menu. Give the modem service a name, and then click Create. Enter the information you received from your service provider, such as the telephone number the modem dials, the account name, and the password.

Click Advanced, and then click PPP.

Select the checkboxes and settings you want to use with your modem.

You can also set other advanced options, such as proxy server settings, depending on the services provided by your Internet account. Click the link below for more information.
