About managing printers

Printer Preferences lets you manage the printers you use. To view Print Preferences, choose RedStar > System Preferences and click Printer.

Adding and deleting printers
To use a printer, it must be in your Printers list. If a printer is connected to your computer, you can add it by clicking Add (+). To delete a printer, select it and click Delete (-).

Managing print jobs
Manage your print jobs by selecting a printer and clicking Open Print Queue. You can pause a printer, delete a print job, hold a print job, and resume a print job.

Setting printer options
If you change options to your printer, you can make sure they're reflected in the Print dialog. Just select the printer, click “Option", and then click Driver.
