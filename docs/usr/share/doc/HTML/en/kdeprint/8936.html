Scaling a document to fit your printer's paper

If you receive a document that's formatted for paper that's a different size from the paper in your printer, you can scale the document to fit. For example, this option can automatically scale an A4 document to fit on 8 1/2 by 11 inch paper.

To scale a document to fit your printer's paper:

Open the document to print, and choose File > Print.

If the Print dialog contains only two pop-up menus and some buttons across the bottom, click the disclosure triangle beside the Printer pop-up menu.

Choose Paper Handling from the print options pop-up menu.

Select “Scale to fit paper size", and choose your printer's paper size from the Destination Paper Size pop-up menu.

If you want to scale the document only if it's too big for the printer's paper, select “Scale down only". If this option is selected and the document is formatted for paper that's smaller than the paper you have, the document is printed at its original size.
