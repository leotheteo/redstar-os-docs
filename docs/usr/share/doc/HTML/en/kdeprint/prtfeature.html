Specifying your printer's features

You can specify the options installed on your printer if the options weren't accurately detected when you added it or if you changed your printer's options after you added it.

To specify your printer's features:

Choose RedStar menu >System Preferences and click Printer.

Select your printer in the Printers list.

Click Option, and then click Driver.

Select the options you want to see when you print.
