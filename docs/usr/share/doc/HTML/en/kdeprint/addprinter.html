Adding a printer

To print to a printer, it must be in the list of printers that appears in the Print dialog's Printer pop-up menu and in Printer preferences. If you connect to a network printer, such as an shared printer, follow a few simple steps to add the printer to your Printers list.

See the manual that came with your printer for specific directions on setting it up.
