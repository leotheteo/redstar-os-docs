Checking the status of a print job

To check the status of a print job:

■

To resume a print job that is on hold, select it and click Resume.

To delete a print job, select it and click Delete.

■

To resume printing after the printer has been paused, click Resume Printer.

To pause printing to this printer, click Pause Printer.

You can't pause or delete print jobs on another user's shared printer.
