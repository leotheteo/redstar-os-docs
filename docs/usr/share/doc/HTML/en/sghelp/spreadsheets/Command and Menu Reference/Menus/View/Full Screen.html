Full Screen

Shows or hides the menus and toolbars in Writer or Calc. To exit the full screen mode, click the Full Screen On/Off button.

To access this command...

Choose View - Full Screen

Shift+Command+J



In Writer and Calc, you can also use the shortcut keys Command+Shift+J to switch between the normal and full screen mode.

You can still use shortcut keys in Full Screen mode, even though the menus are unavailable.
