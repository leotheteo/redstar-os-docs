AutoCorrect

Sets the options for automatically replacing text as you type.

To access this command...

Choose Tools - AutoCorrect



Deactivating Automatic Changes

The AutoCorrect settings are applied when you press the Spacebar after you enter a word.

To turn on or to turn off the AutoCorrect feature, in Sogwang Calc choose Tools - Cell Contents - AutoInput, and in Sogwang Writer choose Format - AutoFormat - While Typing. To apply the AutoFormat settings to an entire text document, choose Format - AutoFormat - Apply.

Replace

Edits the replacement table for automatically correcting or replacing words or abbreviations in your document.

Exceptions

Specify the abbreviations or letter combinations that you do not want Sogwang to correct automatically.

Options

Select the options for automatically correcting errors as you type, and then click OK.

Custom Quotes

Specify the AutoCorrect options for quotation marks in your document.

Back

Resets modified values back to the Sogwangdefault values.
