Slide Show

Switches to normal view where you can create and edit your slides.

To access this command...

Choose Slide Show - Slide Show

F5

You can specify settings for running a slide show in Slide Show - Slide Show Settings.

Specify whether a slide show starts with the current slide or with the first slide on Sogwang Office - Reference - Sogwang Office - General

To start a slide show, do one of the following:

  • Click the Slide Show icon on the Presentation toolbar.

  • Right-click a slide in Normal view and choose Slide Show .

  • Press F5. .

  • Under Windows, right-click the *.sxi or *.odp file in the Windows Explorer, then choose Show .
