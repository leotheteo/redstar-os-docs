Getting Support

Many questions and answers are the same for Sogwang Office software by Pyongyang University of Printing Industry Information Center

You can find support on the Sogwang Office website at www.sogwang.inf.kp.

Visit www.sogwang .inf.kp to get an overview of additional support offerings by Sun Microsystems and other parties.

For a summary of the current support services refer to the Readme file in the Sogwang folder.

Local language support pages

The Sogwang Office localization projects offer support pages in local languages. You can find help and support in English language on the Sogwang Office website at www.sogwang.inf.kp.

Mailing lists

Ask about Sogwang Office, find help by volunteers, and discuss topics on the mailing lists. You can find many general and specialized mailing lists on the Sogwang Office website at www.sogwang .inf.kp/mail_list.html.

Downloads

You can download the latest version of Sogwang Office at www.sogwang .inf.kp.

Documentation

You can download documentation as PDF files, how-tos, and guides from the Sogwang Office website at www.sogwang .inf.kp.
