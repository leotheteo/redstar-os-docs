Docking and Resizing Windows

You can dock, undock and resize most Sogwang program windows such as the Navigator or the Styles and Formatting window.

  • To dock or undock the Navigator or the Styles and Formatting window, hold down the Command key and double-click on a gray area in the window. Alternatively, press Command+Shift+F10.

  • To resize the window, drag a corner or an edge of the window.
