Adding Bullets

  1. Select the paragraph(s) that you want to add bullets to.

  2. On the Formatting Bar, click the Bullets On/Off icon .

To remove bullets, select the bulleted paragraphs, and then click the Bullets On/Off icon on the Formatting Bar.



Formatting Bullets

  • To change the formatting of bulleted list, choose Format - Bullets and Numbering.

    For example, to change the bulleting symbol, click the Options tab, click the browse button (...) next to Character, and then select a special character. You can also click the Graphics tab, and then click a symbol style in the Selection area.
