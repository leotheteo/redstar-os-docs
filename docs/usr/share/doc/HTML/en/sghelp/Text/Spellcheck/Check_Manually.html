Checking Spelling Manually

You can manually check the spelling of a text selection or the entire document.

The spellcheck starts at the current cursor position, or at the beginning of the text selection.

  1. Click in the document, or select the text that you want to check.

  2. Choose Tools - Spellcheck.

  3. When a possible spelling error is encountered, the Spellcheck dialog opens and Sogwang offers some suggested corrections.

  1. Do one of the following:

  • To accept a correction, click the suggestion, and then click Change.

  • Edit the sentence in the upper text box, and then click Change.

  • To add the unknown word to a user-defined dictionary, click Add.
