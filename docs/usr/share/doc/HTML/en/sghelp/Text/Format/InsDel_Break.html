Inserting and Deleting Page Breaks

To insert a manual page break:

  1. Click in your document where you want the new page to begin.

  2. Press Command+Enter.

To delete a manual page break:

  1. Click in front of the first character on the page that follows the manual page break.

  2. Press Backspace.

To delete a manual page break that occurs before a table:

  1. Right-click in the table, and choose Table.

  2. Click the Text Flow tab.

  3. Clear the Break check box.
