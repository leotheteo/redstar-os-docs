Quickly Applying Paragraph Styles

You can use the keyboard to quickly apply the same paragraph style to two consecutive paragraphs.

To apply the paragraph style of the first paragraph to the second paragraph:

  1. Click at the end of the first paragraph.

  2. Press Delete until the second paragraph is added to the end of the first paragraph.

  3. Press Enter.

To apply the paragraph style of the second paragraph to the first paragraph:

  1. Click at the beginning of the second paragraph.

  2. Press Backspace until the second paragraph is added to the end of the first paragraph.

  3. Press Enter.
