Related Topics

Relations

You can choose among various relations to structure your Sogwang Math formula. The relation functions are displayed in the lower part of the Selection window. The list is also in the context menu of the Commands window. All relations that are not contained in the Selection window or in the context menu can be typed manually in the Commands window.
