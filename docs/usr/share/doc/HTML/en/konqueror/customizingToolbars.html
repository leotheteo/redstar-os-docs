Customizing toolbars

Many applications, including the KFinder, Mail, and Safari, provide a set of buttons called the toolbar at the top of windows.

Here are ways to customize toolbars:

■

To minimize a toolbar, hold down the Ctrlkey and click the 타원형 button in the upper-right corner of the window. Keep clicking to cycle through the options for hiding and minimizing the buttons.

■

To hide a toolbar, click the 타원형 button. Click again to show the toolbar.

■

To rearrange or remove buttons, hold down the Ctrlkey and drag the button to a new location, or drag it out of the toolbar.

■

To add or remove buttons, choose View > Customize Toolbar.

A set of buttons appears. Point to each button to see an explanation of what it does. You can do any of the following:

Add a button: Drag it to the toolbar.

Add space between buttons: Drag Separator, Space, or Flexible Space to the toolbar.

Return to default toolbar: Drag the default set to the toolbar.

If the window is too small to show all items in the toolbar, resize the window or click the angle brackets (>>) to reveal remaining buttons.
