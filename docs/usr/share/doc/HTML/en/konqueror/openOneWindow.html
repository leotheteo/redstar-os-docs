Opening more than one window

When you open a folder icon in the KFinder window, it normally opens in the same window, replacing the current contents.

You may need to open more than one window in the KFinder so you can copy or move items from one folder to another, or to create an alias to an item in another folder.

To open an additional window:

Choose File > New KFinder Window, or press the Ctrlkey while you double-click a folder’s icon.

To always open folders in a new window, change your KFinder preferences.
