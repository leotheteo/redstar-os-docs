Customizing windows

KFinder windows are the windows that open when you double-click an icon for a folder or for a device such as the hard disk, or when you click the KFinder icon in the Dock.

There are several ways to customize KFinder windows.

Arranging items
You can decide whether to arrange and view the files and folders in KFinder windows as icons, as a list, or in columns.
■
Arranging items in a KFinder window
Changing the background
You can use a color or a picture as the background for a window.
■
Changing a window’s background
Setting preferences
You can determine the default settings for the look and behavior of KFinder windows when you first open them.
■
Setting KFinder preferences
Scrolling
You can place the scroll arrows together or separate them, and specify what should happen when you click in the scroll bar.
■
Changing how scroll bars work
