Arranging items in a KFinder window

There are four ways to arrange items in a KFinder window: as icons, list, columns, or Cover Flow.

To arrange items:

In the Dock, click the KFinder icon to open a KFinder window.

The KFinder icon is always the first one in the Dock.

In the toolbar along the top of the window, click each of the four View buttons to see icon, list, column, and Cover Flow views. Choose the one you like.

Click the Action (gear) button and choose Show View Options to fine-tune the view.

Each view has a different set of options to fine-tune.

In icon view, to keep icons lined up, open the “Arrange by” menu and choose “Snap to Grid.” A small grid icon appears in the lower left corner of the window.

In list view, to reorder columns drag the column headings back and forth. To resize columns, drag the line between column headers.

In column view, to resize columns drag the bottom of the column divider (where the two vertical lines appear). To resize all columns at once, hold down the Altkey as you drag.
