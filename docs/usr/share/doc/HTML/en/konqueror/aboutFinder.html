About the KFinder

When you start up your computer, the KFinder opens. The KFinder is a program that’s responsible for managing your files, applications, disks, network connections, and devices such as printers. The KFinder is always on when your computer is running, though it may be in the background if you’re using another program such as Mail.

The KFinder provides a menu bar along the top of the screen, and a Dock at the bottom edge. In between the menu bar and Dock are the desktop and any windows you have opened.

The menu bar
At the top of your screen is the menu bar. The leftmost menu is the RedStar menu, designated by an RedStar icon. When you are in the KFinder, you’ll see the KFinder menu next to the RedStar menu. When you use other applications, their menus may appear here instead.
You can click the desktop at any time to get back to the KFinder and see the KFinder menu.
The Dock
By default, the Dock is at the bottom of the screen. You can move the Dock to either side of the screen, and customize it in other ways. The Dock contains icons for applications, such as Mail, that you may use frequently. To open an application, click its icon.
If you click the KFinder (smiling face) icon in the Dock, a window opens and you see the organization of the folders and files on your computer.
The desktop and KFinder windows
Most of the work you do on your computer is in KFinder windows, or in windows for applications you use, such as Mail or the Safari web browser. You can also work directly on the desktop—the background area of your screen. You can organize and customize windows and the desktop to create a comfortable working environment.
