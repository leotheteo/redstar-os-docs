About the sidebar

All KFinder windows have a sidebar that displays icons for things you frequently use, including disks, servers, and folders.

The sidebar is divided into these four sections:

Devices: This section has icons representing your computer, your internal hard disk and any other disks attached to your computer, any CD or DVD discs in your optical drive. You can rearrange the icons in this section, or remove them altogether if you like.

Shared: This section has icons for shared computers and servers on your local network, including AirPort disks. To see all available shared computers and servers, click More. You may need a user name and password to access a shared device.

Places: This section has icons for folders you use most frequently. Your home folder, with a house icon, appears here. Several of the folders that are inside your home folder are also listed here, including Documents, Music, and Pictures.

You can add folders to the Places section. Keeping your favorite folders here is a convenient way to quickly access them.
